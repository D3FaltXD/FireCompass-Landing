What we do

Libraries

We co-develop library collections that are geographically and culturally relevant, as well as scalable, including for: Education in Emergencies, Inclusive Education and Social and Emotional Learning.

Solutions

WRITERS WORKSHOPS

Stories shaped by local voices, for local readers

We co-facilitate Writers Workshops with local authors and illustrators to create books rooted in everyday life, traditions, and local languages and perspectives. These workshops support local publishing ecosystems, prioritise cultural expression, and ensure every child can see themselves in the stories they read.
